About this model

Dataset: Kermany et al. (2018) paediatric chest X-ray dataset, preprocessed with CLAHE contrast enhancement and a custom stratified 80/10/10 split.

Architecture: YOLO26 classification — nano, small, and medium variants were empirically compared, with nano selected for its accuracy-to-efficiency trade-off.

Explainability: Grad-CAM (EigenCAM) highlights the image regions driving each prediction.

Out-of-distribution safeguard: A third "invalid" class, trained on non-X-ray images, flags uploads that aren't chest X-rays.

Research prototype for an MSc dissertation. Not intended for clinical use — always consult a qualified radiologist or physician.
